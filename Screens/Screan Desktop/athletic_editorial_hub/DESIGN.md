## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-leaning, editorial Arabic sports lifestyle hub designed for modern Egyptian athletes, academies, coaches, and sports parents. It unites the curated polish of international editorial performance media (such as Deuce Tennis Analytics) with an ultra-clean, daylight-first digital presence.

### Target Audience & Tone
The experience caters to two primary groups:
1. **Sports Discovery & Consumers (Parents, Swimmers, Fitness Seekers):** Demands effortless visual scanning, trustworthy verified credentials, clear pricing with no hidden fees, and rapid direct actions.
2. **Sports Professionals & Facilities (Coaches, Academies, Pool Centers):** Demands modern, elevated profile presentation that communicates authority, verified track records, and operational clarity.

The emotional signature is **energizing yet calm, authentic, and impeccably organized**. We reject cluttered tables, dense administrative grids, and loud generic promotional banners in favor of airy whitespace, rhythmic cards, tactile bookmark chips, and precise typographic hierarchy.

### Design Movement: High-Contrast Contemporary Editorial
The aesthetic synthesizes:
- **Daylight Minimalism:** A warm alabaster canvas supporting pure daylight white cards.
- **Structural Architectural Contrast:** Anchored by dark slate containers for high-impact hero moments and sticky utility docks.
- **Electric Volt Accents:** Surgical bursts of high-velocity volt-lime reserved strictly for dark surfaces, counterbalanced on daylight surfaces by an organic, accessible deep olive lime.

## Colors

The system operates under a strict **70 / 22 / 8 surface proportion discipline**:

- **70% Primary Canvas (`#F4F5F2`) & Card Daylight (`#FFFFFF`):** Warm Alabaster provides warmth and softens digital eye fatigue compared to harsh sterile blue-whites. Elevated cards sit in pure `#FFFFFF` with hairlines (`#E4E7E5`) to maintain clear spatial grouping.
- **22% Structural Dark (`#2C3437` & `#1A1F21`):** Grounds the interface across hero promotional banners, sticky bottom docks, headers, and high-contrast chips.
- **8% High-Energy Brand Accents (`#B0FE09`, `#3F6212`, `#ECFCCB`):** 
  - `Electric Volt-Lime (#B0FE09)` is calibrated specifically for dark surfaces (`#2C3437` / `#1A1F21`), delivering sharp CTA visibility. It must never appear as unconstrained body text on light surfaces.
  - `Deep Olive Lime (#3F6212)` is the certified WCAG AAA (7.1:1) brand accent for light surfaces, driving active tabs, verified iconography, price tags, and numeric micro-indicators.
  - `Soft Lime Tint (#ECFCCB)` acts as the highlight chip fill, pairing seamlessly with `#3F6212` typography.
- **Action & Direct Channel:** Dedicated `#25D366` green reserved exclusively for instant WhatsApp communication pathways.

## Typography

Typography is bilingual and right-to-left centered (`dir="rtl"`). It resolves the structural baseline differences between geometric Arabic scripts and precise Latin numeric notation.

- **Primary Arabic Script (`Cairo`):** Delivers distinct athletic geometry, balanced descenders, and legible counters for Arabic letterforms across Arabic Egyptian naming and dialectical terminology. Used in weights 400 (Body), 600 (Semibold Badges), and 700 (Titles & Banners).
- **Secondary Latin & Metrics (`Inter`):** Handles all numerical data, currency designations (`EGP` / `ج.م`), percentages, system tags (`#CAP-XXXX`), and time notation to guarantee tabular baseline alignment.
- **RTL Fluidity:** Arabic headlines are lead-aligned right with generous line-height (`1.25` to `1.6`) to accommodate tall Arabic marks and ligatures without clipping.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for mobile-first fluidity at `390px` with responsive expansion to a centered `1280px` max-width desktop container.

### Layout Rhythm
- **RTL Coordinate System:** Margins, padding, and positioning flow strictly right-to-left (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`).
- **Mobile Viewport (390px):** 16px (`1rem`) outer canvas margins. Full-width stacked cards maintain a 16px vertical gap; compact double-column discovery grids utilize a 12px gap.
- **Desktop Viewport (1440px):** 12-column grid system bounded inside 1280px with 24px gutters. A 72px fixed sticky header anchors navigation.

### Horizontal Scroll Trays
Categorical filters, 6-pillar trust badges, and horizontal article strips utilize edge-to-edge overflow with momentum scrolling on touch displays, padded with `1rem` terminal margin to allow visual breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal daylight layering** combined with low-contrast containment rather than heavy, drop-shadowed layers.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F4F5F2` Warm Alabaster background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FFFFFF` pure white fill with a subtle perimeter hairline (`1px solid #E4E7E5`) and an ultra-diffused, ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px rgba(44, 52, 55, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Docks & Overlays):** Sticky bottom mobile navigation bars, bottom sheets, and modal action dialogs sit at `0 12px 36px rgba(26, 31, 33, 0.12)` with `1px solid rgba(228, 231, 229, 0.8)`.
- **Level 3 (Dark Anchors & Hero Blocks):** High-contrast dark surfaces (`#2C3437`) that establish structural depth without casting blur shadows, functioning as solid contrast anchors against light surroundings.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **curved geometric radius discipline** that reflects athletic tracks, swimming lane decks, and modern consumer hardware.

- **Primary Cards & Hero Banners:** `20px` (`rounded-xl` equivalent for hero containers) and `16px` (`rounded-lg` for standard content and facility cards).
- **Secondary Modules & Thumbnails:** `12px` radius for article preview images, modal insets, and inner card blocks.
- **Interactive Elements & Pills:** Full pill geometry (`9999px`) for search bars, action buttons, category filter tabs, bookmark chips, and trust badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Hero CTA (Dark Context Only):** Electric Volt-Lime (`#B0FE09`) background with Dark Slate (`#2C3437`) Cairo Bold text. Height: `48px`, shape: pill (`9999px`).
- **Primary Daylight CTA (Light Context):** Deep Olive Lime (`#3F6212`) background with `#FFFFFF` Cairo Bold text.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action Button:** Official WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`) or Captenly Dark Slate (`#2C3437`) featuring a `#25D366` / `#B0FE09` icon accompaniment. Displays structured pre-filled action text like `تواصل عبر واتساب 💬`.
- **Bookmark & Secondary Icon Buttons:** Pure `#FFFFFF` or frosted dark circles (`36px` to `40px`) containing the bookmark badge (`🔖`) or filter sliders (`⚙️`), outlined with `#E4E7E5`.

### Chips & Trust Badges
- **Category Filter Pills:** Inactive state features `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` with a hairline border `#E4E7E5` and `#5A6569` text. Active state uses `#ECFCCB` fill with `#3F6212` text or `#2C3437` fill with `#B0FE09` indicator.
- **6 Signature Trust Badges:** Compact horizontal pills (`12px` Cairo SemiBold) with Soft Lime Tint (`#ECFCCB`) backgrounds and `#3F6212` typography (e.g., `✓ هوية موثقة`, `✓ شهادة اتحاد السباحة`).

### Cards
- **Discovery Card (Coach / Facility):** Pure Daylight White surface (`16px` radius, `1px solid #E4E7E5`), `16:10` photography, integrated top-left bookmark button, top-right verification badge, Cairo Bold title, star rating, and price highlighted in Deep Olive (`#3F6212`).
- **Training School / Program Card:** Dedicated card featuring structured specification pills (sessions, age, pool specs), price with "no hidden fees" indicator, and pre-formatted reference code (`#CAP-XXXX`).
- **SEO & Article Card (Editorial Strip):** Horizontal layout on mobile with an `88px × 88px` rounded-12 thumbnail, editorial category pill, Cairo Bold title, estimated reading time, and bookmark trigger.

### Input Fields & Search
- Full-width pill search input (`48px` height) in `#FFFFFF` with `#E4E7E5` border and `#5A6569` placeholder text. The search icon anchors the right edge (RTL origin) and filter settings trigger on the left edge.

### Navigation Bars
- **Desktop:** Centered 72px sticky bar, logo right, search/location center, bookmark/profile left.
- **Mobile Bottom Dock:** Elevated floating pill dock containing 5 core destinations: Home (`الرئيسية`), Explore (`استكشف`), Favorites (`المفضلة 🔖`), Ledger/Publish (`حصصي` / `إدارة ونشر`), and Account (`حسابي`).
